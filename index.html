Lilypad Hopper

Privacy Policy · Last updated October 25, 2026

Privacy

Lilypad Hopper does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • No internet connection is used.

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, best scores, chosen frog and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent anywhere, and deleting the app deletes it.

The game reads your device's motion sensor (accelerometer) only to steer the frog while you play. This data is not saved or sent anywhere.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
